RoomBreaker for Windows
=======================

Plugin (VST3)
  1. Copy the folder "RoomBreaker.vst3" to
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  2. Rescan plugins in your DAW (Ableton Live, Cubase, FL Studio, Reaper, Bitwig, Studio One...).
     RoomBreaker appears under the manufacturer "RoomBreaker", category Reverb.

Standalone
  RoomBreaker.exe runs without a DAW. Pick your audio device in its Options menu.

Sidechain
  The plugin has an optional stereo sidechain input. In the Engine page, set
  "What breaks the walls" to Sidechain or Both, then route a track to the
  plugin's sidechain in your DAW.

These builds are not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask for confirmation
the first time the standalone app is started.
